# Proposed MS in Biostatistics at UNCW

Thank you for reviewing materials related to the proposed Master of Science in Biostatistics at the University of North Carolina Wilmington.

This repository contains planning materials for a proposed applied graduate program in biostatistics, focused on clinical research and related health science settings. The program is currently moving through the planning and approval process.

## What is here

This repository currently includes:

1. **Planning proposal**
   - Current draft of the MS Biostatistics planning document.
   - This document describes the proposed program structure, rationale, delivery model, workforce alignment, and approval pathway.

2. **Support letter guidance**
   - A brief guide for industry partners who may be willing to provide a letter of support.
   - The guidance highlights the kinds of points that are most useful in letters, especially workforce need, program relevance, and why UNCW is a meaningful partner for this effort.

3. **Faculty-Industry Exchange**
   - This is an idea that may be included in the planning proposal but is not in currently (it will be, at most, the one paragraph insert shown at the end of the document)
   - I would like feedback on the feasibility and value of such a program, separate from any other support for the moment.

## Why I am sharing this

At this stage, I am asking selected industry and professional partners to review the planning materials and consider whether they can provide support for the proposal.

The most helpful support will come from people who can speak from an employer or managerial perspective in biostatistics. Letters from company leaders, managers, directors, team leads, or senior professionals are especially valuable.

## What is most useful

If you are willing to review the materials, the most useful feedback or support would address:

- whether the proposed program responds to a real workforce need;
- whether the applied clinical research focus is valuable;
- whether the proposed online and flexible format would matter for working professionals or employers;
- why UNCW would be a meaningful place to build this program;
- and whether you or your organization might be willing to remain connected as the program develops.

## Support letters

If you are willing to provide, or help coordinate, a support letter, please review the support letter guidance file included in this repository.

A support letter does not need to be long. The strongest letters are specific about why this proposed program matters and why UNCW is an appropriate partner in developing it.
